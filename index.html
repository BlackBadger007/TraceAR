How to use TraceAR

  1. Place your device above your paper or drawing surface.
  2. Press Start camera and allow camera permission.
  3. Upload a reference image from your device.
  4. Drag the image over your paper, then adjust size, rotation and opacity.
  5. Trace the visible guide with a pencil or pen.

This is a camera-overlay tracing tool, not full spatial AR. For camera access, open the site on HTTPS or localhost.
